FAQ

Answers to questions about the Trailmark project.

What is Trailmark?

Trailmark is a static web project — a curated guide to all 63 U.S. National Parks. Each park has its own illustrated SVG badge and a dedicated detail page covering history, key facts, and what makes it worth visiting.

How many parks are listed right now?

Yosemite is the first fully built park page. All 63 parks are planned. New parks are added gradually — with care given to each badge design and write-up rather than just populating a template.

Can I suggest a park or report an error?

Yes. The project is open on GitHub. Open an issue to suggest a correction, report a problem, or propose a feature. Contributions are welcome.

Is Trailmark affiliated with the National Park Service?

No. Trailmark is an independent project made for personal and educational use. It has no official affiliation with the NPS or any government agency.
