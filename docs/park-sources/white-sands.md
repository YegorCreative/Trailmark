# White Sands sources

Live pages opened:

- Establishment, last updated September 25, 2023: https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/management/establishment-of-white-sands-national-park.htm
  - National park on December 20, 2019. Monument abolished and incorporated.
  - Monument had protected parts of the 275-square-mile gypsum dunefield.
  - Dunehearth mounds, largest collection of Ice Age trackways, human footprints 21,000–23,000 years old.
- News release, December 21, 2019 (archived): https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/news/nr12212019.htm
  - Monument established January 18, 1933, by Hoover.
  - World's largest gypsum dunefield, gypsum hearthmounds found nowhere else, largest collection of Ice Age fossilized footprints, more than 10,000 years of human presence.
- Nature, last updated August 21, 2020: https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/index.htm
  - 275 square miles, largest gypsum dunefield, park preserves more than half.
  - More than 800 animal species. Cyanobacteria crust between dunes.
  - Lake Otero tracks: Columbian mammoths, giant sloths, dire wolves. Some trackways run two miles.
  - Salt cedar up to 200 gallons a day.
