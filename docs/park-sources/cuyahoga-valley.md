# Cuyahoga Valley sources

Live pages used:

- History and culture: https://www.nps.gov/cuva/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Authorized December 27, 1974. Officially designated June 26, 1975. 1969 river fire just north of the park. Removal of Native nations. Connecticut Western Reserve.
- Park basics: https://www.nps.gov/cuva/learn/kidsyouth/park-basics.htm
  - More than fifty square miles. 26 miles of river. Ka-ih-ogh-ha. People for 13,000 years. Renamed a national park in 2000. Recreation area in 1974.
- Press kit: https://www.nps.gov/cuva/learn/news/presskit.htm
  - About 33,000 acres and about 32,950 acres. Name-change legislation October 11, 2000, P.L. 106-291. Enabling legislation December 27, 1974. Federal and non-federal acreage split. About 250 historic structures.
- 50th anniversary: https://www.nps.gov/cuva/cvnp50.htm
  - Public Law 93-555, December 27, 1974.
- Homepage note of more than 125 miles of trail: https://www.nps.gov/cuva/index.htm

The beaver drawing is not a census. The waterfall in the header is not given a proper name. Fees and closures are left to NPS.
