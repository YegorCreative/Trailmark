# Hawaiʻi Volcanoes sources

Live pages used:

- Fact sheet, last updated May 15, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/news/hawaii-volcanoes-national-park-fact-sheet.htm
  - Established August 1, 1916. 354,461 acres. Sea level to 13,681 feet. 123,100 acres legislated wilderness. Biosphere Reserve 1980. World Heritage 1987. Seven ecological zones. Seven threatened species including nēnē. 47 endangered species including honuʻea.
- Name act: https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/management/19600913_fed_law.htm
  - Present name effective on enactment, approved September 22, 1961. Original act August 1, 1916.
- Kīlauea: https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/nature/kilauea.htm
  - Youngest and most active volcano on the island. Almost 90 percent of the surface covered in 1,000 years. Formed underwater roughly 280,000 years ago. Pelehonuamea and Halemaʻumaʻu.
- Places: https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/historyculture/places.htm
  - More than five centuries of Native Hawaiian use before the park. Puʻuloa, footprints, Wahaʻula buried by lava. Kahuku 16,451 acres.
- World Heritage page, last updated January 27, 2021: https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/management/world_heritage_site.htm
  - Older figures: 333,086 acres and 13,677 feet. Not used. The 2025 fact sheet is the statistics page.

The turtle drawing is a honuʻea, not a count. Eruption status is left to NPS.
