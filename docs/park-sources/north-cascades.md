# North Cascades sources

Live pages used:

- Glaciers / Glacial Features: https://www.nps.gov/noca/learn/nature/glaciers.htm
  - "Over 300 glaciers and countless snowfields," the most heavily glaciated area in the United States outside Alaska. Most have shrunk dramatically in the last century from reduced precipitation and warmer summers. Banded Glacier shown retreating 1960-2016. Glacier Monitoring Program tracks changes. Disappearing glaciers affect soil development, vegetation, and salmon-supporting aquatic ecosystems.
- Park homepage: https://www.nps.gov/noca/index.htm
  - "More than 300 glaciers." Lake Chelan and Ross Lake named as destinations within the park experience (Stehekin at Lake Chelan's head; camping at Ross Lake).
- Park statistics page (https://www.nps.gov/noca/learn/management/statistics.htm): returned an "in-progress" placeholder with no data at the time of research; not used as a source.
- WebSearch summary citing the NPS Foundation Document for the North Cascades National Park Complex: national park itself about 505,000 acres in north and south units; Ross Lake National Recreation Area about 117,000 acres, wedged between the park's units; Lake Chelan National Recreation Area about 62,000 acres, abutting the park's southern boundary.
- WebSearch summary of NPS Geodiversity Atlas: complex total about 276,928 hectares / 684,305 acres; established October 2, 1968.
- WebSearch summary re: American pika, citing NPS pika-project and related pages: pikas build haypiles and do not hibernate; pika abundance declined at lower elevations in a record-low-snowpack year while rising slightly at higher, snow-retaining elevations. Marmots on Sahale Mountain studied alongside pikas as climate-sensitive species.

No dedicated NPS statistics page was available for North Cascades at the time of this research; the Foundation Document acreage split (park vs. the two national recreation areas) is used instead, cited above as the closest available breakdown, and the essay notes this limitation directly rather than presenting the figures as from a dedicated statistics page.
