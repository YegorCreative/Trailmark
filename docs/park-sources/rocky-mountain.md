# Rocky Mountain sources

Live pages opened:

- People, last updated March 22, 2022: https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/historyculture/people.htm
  - Dedication photograph dated September 4, 1915: Enos Mills, F. O. Stanley, Ed Taylor, Mary Belle King Sherman, Governor George Carlson.
  - Isabella Bird, A Lady's Life in the Rocky Mountains (1879), quoted by NPS: "The scenery is the most glorious I have ever seen, and is above us, around us, at every door."
  - Mary Belle King Sherman, the Burnell sisters, Imogene Green MacPhearson and Moraine Park Lodge, Margaret Fuller Boos in 1928.
- History and culture index, last updated December 1, 2015: https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Collections hold over 44,000 items. Information office hours and the Trail Ridge Road recording are printed there.
- NPS education timeline PDF: https://home.nps.gov/romo/learn/education/upload/Cultural-History-Of-RMNP-Teacher-Guide-Final.pdf
  - Legislation signed January 26, 1915, and dedicated September 4.
  - Stanley Hotel opened July 4, 1909. Fall River Road crews met in September 1920. Grand Ditch and east-side mines noted.
