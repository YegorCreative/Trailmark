# Grand Teton sources

Live pages opened:

- Management: https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/management/index.htm
  - Established 1929. Jackson Hole National Monument 1943. Combined as the present park in 1950. Rockefeller parkway established 1972.
- Nature and science, last updated April 23, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/nature/index.htm
  - Range rises more than 7,000 feet above Jackson Hole. Valley floor 6,320 feet. Grand Teton summit 13,770 feet.
  - Sagebrush valley, forests, wildflowers, cirque lakes, moraine lakes, Snake River from north of the park.
  - Snow often early November through May. Coldest recorded temperature minus 63 Fahrenheit.
  - Wildlife page phrase: tiniest shrew to largest grizzly.
- History and culture, last updated April 17, 2026: https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Cultural history more than 10,000 years. Menors Ferry Historic District. Expansion in 1950 by adding the monument.
- Creation account (NPS PDF): https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/upload/creation.pdf
  - February 26, 1929: Coolidge signed a 96,000-acre park of the range and six glacial lakes.
  - September 14, 1950: Truman signed the merger into about 310,000 acres. Rockefeller land transferred December 16, 1949.
- Cultural history: https://www.nps.gov/grte/learn/historyculture/cultural.htm
  - 1929 park was the range and lakes. 1943 monument. 1949 donation. 1950 present park. Dude ranching in the 1920s.

The barn drawing is a valley type, not a named homestead. Fees and closures are left to NPS.
