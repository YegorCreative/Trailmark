# Petrified Forest sources

Live page opened:

- Frequently asked questions, last updated April 26, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/pefo/faqs.htm
  - National monument in 1906, in the second group with Montezuma Castle and El Morro. National park in 1962 by act of Congress.
  - Currently over 200,000 acres. A 2004 law authorizes expansion up to 218,533 acres.
  - The whole park is inside the Painted Desert. Wood is in place, not imported. Collecting is illegal.
  - Ancestral Puebloan is the term NPS asks for. Puerco Pueblo, solstice marker, Coelophysis, Chindesaurus.
  - Badlands, bentonite, ash about 200 million years old, monsoon loss up to about a quarter inch a year.
  - Pronghorn, elk, mule deer, coyotes, bobcats, golden eagles. Pronghorn are not antelope.
  - Park drive 28 miles. Rainfall about 10 inches. The park closes at night.
