# Saguaro sources

Live pages opened:

- History and culture, last updated January 7, 2026: https://www.nps.gov/sagu/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Hoover proclamation, March 1, 1933. National park designation, 1994.
  - Tucson Mountain District, about 15,360 acres, added November 15, 1961.
  - Signal Hill. Freeman Homestead and two of six lime kilns on the Arizona State Register.
  - Manning Cabin, 1905, about 12 miles, backcountry permit.
  - Happy Valley Lookout, staffed in summer through the 1970s.
  - Archeological record of more than 8,000 years, including Hohokam. Traditional use area for saguaro fruit.
- Nature, last updated May 6, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/sagu/learn/nature/index.htm
  - Sonoran Desert called the most biodiverse desert. Rincon elevations 2,670 to 8,666 feet and six biotic communities. Precipitation about 12.30 inches.
  - Tucson Mountain District 2,180 to 4,687 feet, desert scrub and grassland, about 10.27 inches.
  - Rincon animals named: black bear, Mexican spotted owl, Arizona mountain kingsnake, white-tailed deer.
  - Tucson Mountain animals named: coyote, Gambel's quail, desert tortoise.
