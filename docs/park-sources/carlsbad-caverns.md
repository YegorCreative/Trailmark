- History and culture, last updated May 6, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/cave/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Carlsbad Cave National Monument, October 25, 1923. Carlsbad Caverns National Park, May 14, 1930.
  - One of more than 300 limestone caves in a fossil reef from an inland sea about 265 million years ago.
  - People in the Guadalupe Mountains 12,000 to 14,000 years ago. Cooking rings and pictographs inside the park.
  - Early visitors entered in a guano-mining bucket.
  - Cavern Historic District and Rattlesnake Springs Historic District.
  - Museum and archives of approximately one million cultural-resource artifacts.
