# Mount Rainier sources

Live pages used:

- Press kit: https://www.nps.gov/mora/learn/news/presskit.htm
  - Total authorized boundary 236,381 acres (plus 140 acres outside current boundary near Carbon River). 97% designated wilderness. Summit 14,410 feet; park elevation range about 1,700 to 14,410 feet. 28 named glaciers covering 35 square miles, the largest single-mountain glacial system in the contiguous 48 states. Volcanic base spreads about 100 square miles, rising 9,000-11,000 feet above valley floors 3-6 miles away.
- Mount Rainier: America's Fifth National Park: https://www.nps.gov/mora/learn/management/fifth-park.htm
  - Established March 2, 1899, the fifth-oldest national park. Does not name the signing president.
- Mount Rainier history: https://www.nps.gov/mora/learn/historyculture/mount-rainier-history.htm
  - Indigenous names recorded: Tahoma, Takhoma, Tacoma, Ta-co-bet, Taqo ma, Tkobed, Taqo bid, Tkomen, Nutselip, Pshwawanoapami-tahoma. Cowlitz, Muckleshoot, Nisqually, Puyallup, Squaxin Island, Yakama, and Coast Salish nations named as stewards; documented use at least 9,000 years. First recorded non-Native ascent 1870 by P.B. Van Trump and General Hazard Stevens, guided to base camp by Sluiskin. James Longmire discovered a mineral spring in 1883 descending from his own summit attempt, later built a hotel/spa. Lobbying campaign began 1893; Congress passed the park bill in 1899.
- Glaciers page: https://www.nps.gov/mora/learn/nature/glaciers.htm
  - 28 major glaciers plus numerous unnamed snow/ice patches, covering about 30 square miles (this figure superseded by the press kit's 35 square miles, used instead as the more dedicated statistics source). Emmons Glacier largest at 4.3 square miles. Carbon Glacier has the lowest terminus elevation, 3,600 feet, of any glacier in the continental US. Retreat documented since 1896.
- WebSearch summary of NPS results: research on marmot colonies at Mount Rainier records average colony size about 10.8 (rounded to about 11 in the essay).

Conflicting figures: glaciers.htm gives glacier coverage as "about 30 square miles"; the press kit gives "35 square miles." The press kit, the more dedicated statistics-style source, is used. Acreage of 235,625 (from an earlier search snippet) vs. 236,381 (press kit) is resolved in favor of the press kit.
