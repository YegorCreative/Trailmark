# Big Bend sources

Live pages opened:

- Natural history, last updated November 16, 2023: https://www.nps.gov/bibe/learn/nature/index.htm
  - More than 1,200 plant species, about 60 cacti, 11 amphibians, 56 reptiles, 40 fish, 75 mammals, over 400 birds, about 3,600 insects.
  - More types of birds, bats, butterflies, ants, scorpions, and cacti than any other U.S. national park.
  - Rocks from 500-million-year-old stone at Persimmon Gap to windblown dunes at Boquillas Canyon.
  - Marine and continental sedimentary rocks, volcanic rocks, and evidence of three North American mountain-building episodes.
  - 130 million years of life preserved in the rocks. Water defines the desert.
- Fossils in the first 50 years of the National Park Service: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/fossils/1916-1966-first-50-years-of-the-national-park-service.htm
  - Authorized June 20, 1935 (Public Law 74-157). Established June 12, 1944, after the deed of conveyance. Ross Maxwell, superintendent 1944–1952.
  - Museum fire December 24, 1941, in the Chisos Basin. WPA quarries 1938–1939; more than 500 bones under William Strain. Deinosuchus jaws collected by Brown and Bird.
