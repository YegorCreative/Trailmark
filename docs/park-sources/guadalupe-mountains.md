# Guadalupe Mountains sources

Live pages opened:

- History and culture, last updated September 15, 2021: https://www.nps.gov/gumo/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - More than 10,000 years of human history. Mescalero Apache and Buffalo Soldiers. Butterfield Overland Mail.
  - Frijole Ranch, Williams Ranch, Pinery Station. Collecting artifacts is illegal.
  - Fall 2022 was the fiftieth anniversary of establishment in 1972.
- Nature, last updated June 8, 2022: https://www.nps.gov/gumo/learn/nature/index.htm
  - El Capitan as the cliff seen from the road. Permian reef about 265 million years old, about 400 miles long.
  - More than 1,000 plant species, 60 mammals, 289 birds, 55 reptiles.
  - Life zones: Chihuahuan desert, oak and maple woodlands, ponderosa pine and Douglas fir.
  - Salt Basin Dunes appear as a named photograph. No dune measurement was copied because the caption does not give one.

Guadalupe Peak elevation and "highest point in Texas" are not stated. The ring-tailed animal in the extra drawing is not claimed as a species record.
