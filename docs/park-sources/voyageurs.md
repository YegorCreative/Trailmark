# Voyageurs sources

Live pages used:

- "Voyageurs National Park Celebrates 50th Birthday": https://home.nps.gov/voya/learn/news/voyageurs-national-park-celebrates-50th-birthday.htm
  - Established 1975, signed into law by President Richard Nixon. Legislation text: preserves "the outstanding scenery, geological conditions, and waterway system which constituted a part of the historic route of the Voyageurs who contributed significantly to the opening of the Northwestern United States." Named for French-Canadian voyageur fur traders. 218,000+ acres.
- WebSearch summary of NPS results: nearly 84,000 acres of water, about 39% of the park's 218,000 total acres, across 4 major lakes and 26 interior lakes.
- Dark Sky Park Designation: https://www.nps.gov/voya/learn/nature/dark-sky-park-designation.htm
  - In 2019, the park worked with the International Dark-Sky Association toward designation; goal to raise night-friendly lighting from 25% to 68% immediately, with a long-term goal of 100% within a decade. Exact year of full IDA designation not stated on this page, so not asserted in the essay.
- Gray wolf: https://www.nps.gov/voya/learn/nature/gray-wolf.htm
  - Population "between 30-50 wolves, split into 6 to 9 packs," most years within that range; 2-3 territories generally on the remote Kabetogama Peninsula. Dominant predator; primarily feeds on deer, moose, and beaver.

A WebSearch summary separately cited "about 6-8 wolf packs with a total of 35-45 wolves"; the essay uses the range from the directly fetched gray-wolf.htm page (30-50 wolves, 6-9 packs) as the more authoritative, directly sourced figure.
