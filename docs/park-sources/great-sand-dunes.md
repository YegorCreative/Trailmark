# Great Sand Dunes sources

Live page opened:

- Frequently asked questions, last updated June 8, 2026: https://www.nps.gov/grsa/faqs.htm
  - Tallest dunes in North America. Star Dune 750 feet base to crest. High Dune 699 feet from its base, crest higher above sea level than Star Dune.
  - Main dunefield about 30 square miles. Park and preserve about 150,000 acres.
  - Elevation 7,520 to 13,604 feet (Tijeras Peak). Visitor center 8,170 feet.
  - Monument in 1932 by Hoover, dunefield only. National park and preserve in 2004.
  - Six endemic insects, including the Great Sand Dunes tiger beetle.
  - Dunes wilderness 1976. Sangre de Cristo wilderness 1993. No motorized vehicles on the dunefield.
  - Sand can exceed 150°F. No scorpions, venomous snakes, or quicksand.
  - Dating is inconclusive. NPS says probably thousands of years, not millions.
  - Medano Creek timing and the sand-recycling pattern.
