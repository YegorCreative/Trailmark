# Channel Islands sources

Live page opened:

- Establishing Channel Islands National Park, last updated February 17, 2023: https://www.nps.gov/chis/learn/historyculture/park-history.htm
  - Public Law 96-199, signed by President Jimmy Carter on March 5, 1980.
  - Monument: President Franklin D. Roosevelt, April 26, 1938, Anacapa and Santa Barbara. Proclamation names Pleistocene elephant fossils, ancient trees, volcanism, deposition, and sea erosion.
  - Truman proclamation No. 2825, February 9, 1949: 17,635 acres, water within one nautical mile of Anacapa and Santa Barbara.
  - San Miguel remains U.S. Navy property, managed by NPS. Santa Rosa purchased from Vail and Vickers in December 1986. Santa Cruz: partial Gherini purchase in the 1990s; The Nature Conservancy donated 8,500 acres in 2000; 24 percent public ownership.
  - Island Packers began service about a year after the monument received its own superintendent in May 1967.

A 2020 news release with species totals was not used. The fox and the seal in the art are drawings, not censuses. Boat schedules are left to NPS.
