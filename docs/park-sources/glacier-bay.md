# Glacier Bay sources

Live pages used:

- Fact sheet: https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/management/glacier-bay-fact-sheet.htm
  - Monument February 26, 1925. Park and preserve December 2, 1980. Biosphere reserve 1986. World Heritage Site 1992.
- History and culture: https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Vancouver 1794. Muir, ice retreating as fast as a mile a year. Cooper. Monument February 26, 1925. Park December 2, 1980.
- From monument to park: https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/historyculture/from-monument-to-park-1980.htm
  - ANILCA extended the boundary toward the Alsek and Dry Bay. World Heritage with Wrangell-St. Elias and Kluane.
- William S. Cooper: https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/historyculture/william-s-cooper-a-vision-of-preservation.htm
  - Visit in 1916. Bare ground to spruce and hemlock in about a century. Monument 1925 as a park for science. Plots still studied. A cut-off sentence about the plots being "the oldest" was not used.
- Monument formation: https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/historyculture/monument-formation.htm
  - Mining opened 1936. Mining in the Parks Act 1976. Brady Glacier claim. 1939 expansion more than doubled the monument. Native seal hunting ended 1974. Gustavus homesteaders.
- Natural heritage: https://www.nps.gov/glba/natural-heritage.htm
  - Coolidge proclamation, February 26, 1925, quoted for tidewater glaciers and forests of different ages.
- Management: https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/management/index.htm
  - Purpose statement. Monument 1925, park 1980. Homeland and laboratory.

The whale and the seal are drawings, not counts. Boat rules are left to NPS.
