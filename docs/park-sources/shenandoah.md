# Shenandoah sources

Live pages used:

- WebSearch summary of NPS results: established December 26, 1935, first wilderness area east of the Mississippi to become a national park. Over 200,000 acres; 79,579 acres (about 40%) designated wilderness; more than 60 peaks over 3,000 feet. Skyline Drive construction began 1931 under the Hoover administration, an NPS/Bureau of Public Roads project, cost more than $4,500,000, 105 miles long, park never more than about 15 miles wide, 199,045 total acres.
- The Displaced: https://www.nps.gov/shen/learn/historyculture/displaced.htm
  - Park assembled from over 3,000 individual land tracts, purchased or condemned by the Commonwealth of Virginia. At least 500 families displaced in the 1930s. By spring 1938: 42 elderly residents given life estates, 175 families relocated to resettlement communities, some evicted with houses burned, most left on their own. By 1995, only 14 buildings with wooden components remained standing in Nicholson Hollow.
- WebSearch summary re: CCC: 12 CCC camps in 9 locations, May 1933-March 1942, about 200 men per camp, more than 6,500 total.
- Animals: https://www.nps.gov/shen/learn/nature/animals.htm
  - More than 50 mammal species (white-tailed deer, bobcat, big brown bat named). Over 190 resident/transient bird species. 26 reptile species including eastern box turtle, timber rattlesnake, broad-headed skink. 41 fish species including brook trout. Endangered Shenandoah Salamander named. Bison and elk hunted to regional extinction by 1798 and 1855 respectively; most returned species reintroduced elsewhere in Virginia or recovered naturally, not specifically within the park.
- Old Rag Circuit: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/old-rag-circuit.htm
  - 9.4-mile circuit, 2,348 feet elevation gain, Very Strenuous, adventurous rock scramble, $1 day-use ticket required March 1-November 30 plus standard entrance pass, 7-8 hours typical.
- Dark Hollow Falls: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/dark-hollow-falls.htm
  - 1.4-mile circuit, 0.75-mile descent, about 1h15m round trip, trailhead at Skyline Drive mile 50.7 (Central District). Height of falls not stated on this page.
