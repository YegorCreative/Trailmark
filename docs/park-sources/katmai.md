# Katmai sources

Live pages used:

- Centennial blog: https://www.nps.gov/katm/blogs/katmai-a-99-year-old-gem.htm
  - Proclamation September 24, 1918. Original monument over one million acres.
- 2018 centennial: https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/2018-centennial.htm
  - Novarupta, 1912, called the largest volcanic eruption of the twentieth century. Valley of Ten Thousand Smokes. National park in 1980. About 4 million acres.
- Presidents blog: https://www.nps.gov/katm/blogs/u-s-presidents-and-katmai.htm
  - Wilson 1918. Carter added 1.3 million acres in 1978 and signed ANILCA December 2, 1980. 3.4 million acres of wilderness. Hunting in the preserve.
- Nature: https://www.nps.gov/katm/learn/nature/index.htm
  - Most fumaroles have cooled. More than 700 plant species. Bears became a reason for later boundaries.
- Homepage, last updated November 17, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/katm/
  - 9,000 years of human history. Salmon and thousands of brown bears.
- Learn page: at least 9,000 years. Agrees with the homepage.
- History: https://www.nps.gov/katm/learn/historyculture/history.htm
  - Brooks Camp added 1931. Bear watching grew in the 1980s. Peterson's lodges in the 1950s.

No separate statistics page was found, so the centennial's 4 million acres is the size used. The 1918 monument's "over one million" is a different boundary. The salmon drawing is not a run size. Bear-viewing distance and flights are left to NPS.
