# Badlands sources

Live pages opened:

- Nature, last updated July 31, 2020: https://www.nps.gov/badl/learn/nature/index.htm
  - 244,000 acres of buttes, pinnacles, and spires. Mixed-grass prairie between tall-grass and short-grass.
  - Rocks deposited as early as 75 million years ago. Erosion began about 500,000 years ago.
  - Wildlife from bison to butterflies. No population in that sentence.
- History and culture, last updated July 8, 2020: https://www.nps.gov/badl/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - People about 12,000 years. Links for the Lakota name, the Dawes Act, the World War II aerial gunnery range on Pine Ridge, and homesteading. No establishment year on this index.
- Geodiversity Atlas, last updated June 17, 2024: https://www.nps.gov/articles/nps-geodiversity-atlas-badlands-national-park-south-dakota.htm
  - Monument 1939. Acreage added 1976. National park 1978. No day for 1978.
  - 242,756 acres on this page. More than 250 vertebrate species. White River Group fossil counts as used in the essay.
- Naming article: https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/mako-sica.htm
  - Lakota mako sica. French les mauvaises terres a traveser. Wet clay. A 1922 proposal would have used the name Wonderland. That last sentence is not used in the essay.

The bighorn drawing is not a census. The two acreage figures are both recorded and not averaged.
