# Great Basin sources

Live pages opened:

- Park homepage, updated September 18, 2026: https://www.nps.gov/grba/index.htm
  - Wheeler Peak 13,063 feet. Bristlecone pines, Lehman Caves (stalactites, stalagmites, popcorn, cave shields), dark skies.
- Management, updated October 8, 2021: https://www.nps.gov/grba/learn/management/index.htm
  - Established October 27, 1986. Lehman Caves National Monument abolished and incorporated.
  - Over 77,000 acres, and 77,100 acres stated in the mission text. Wheeler Peak, highest peak contained within Nevada.
  - Over 660 species. Bristlecones in excess of 4,000 years. More than 45 caves. The only glacier in the Great Basin. Visibility often exceeding 120 miles.
  - Cultural resources from about 12,000 years ago through mining and ranching.
- The Prometheus story, updated January 5, 2021: https://www.nps.gov/grba/learn/historyculture/the-prometheus-story.htm
  - Cut in 1964 with Forest Service permission. 4,862 rings, estimated about 4,900 years. A White Mountains bristlecone dated in 2012 at 5,065 years.
- People, updated April 22, 2021: https://www.nps.gov/grba/learn/historyculture/people.htm
  - Absalom Lehman ranch in the 1870s, cave discovered 1885. Johnson Lake Mine inside the park. Osceola outside. Orchard trees remain near the visitor center.

The cave drawing matches the formations named on the homepage. No bristlecone age was invented beyond the pages above.
