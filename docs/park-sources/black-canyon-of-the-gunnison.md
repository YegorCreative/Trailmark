# Black Canyon of the Gunnison sources

Live pages opened:

- 20th anniversary release, October 10, 2019: https://www.nps.gov/blca/learn/news/pr19-14.htm
  - President Clinton signed the park act on October 21, 1999, enlarging the 1933 national monument.
  - Sponsors: Senator Ben Nighthorse Campbell and Congressman Scott McGinnis.
  - 2017: 2,494 acres of private inholding added with Land and Water Conservation Fund money.
- Nature, last updated May 19, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/blca/learn/nature/index.htm
  - Gunnison River, wind, and weathering over about two million years.
  - Pinyon-juniper, Gambel oak, inner canyon, and riparian habitats.
- Geology, last updated April 24, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/blca/learn/nature/geology.htm
  - Gunnison Uplift about 60 million years ago raised 1.8-billion-year-old rock. Volcanic burial about 30 million years ago. River cutting as early as two million years ago.
  - Greatest depth 2,722 feet just north of Warner Point. Narrowest width 40 feet at the river. Length 48 miles, 14 inside the park. Average descent 43 feet per mile.
  - Not the steepest, deepest, or narrowest canyon in the West, but a strong combination of all three.
  - Eastern edge of the Colorado Plateau, beside the Southern Rockies.
