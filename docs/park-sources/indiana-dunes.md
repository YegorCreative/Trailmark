# Indiana Dunes sources

Live pages used:

- History: https://www.nps.gov/indu/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Public Law 89-761, November 5, 1966, 8,330 acres. Expansions 1976, 1980, 1986, 1992, to more than 15,000 acres. Name change February 15, 2019, Public Law 116-6. 61st national park. Cowles, Douglas, Buell. Kennedy Compromise. Not all authorized land acquired.
- Statistics, last updated June 2, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/indu/learn/management/statistics.htm
  - Fiscal year 2025 acreage 16,035. Earlier fiscal years differ. This is the statistics page, so the essay uses 16,035.
- News of the rename: https://www.nps.gov/indu/learn/news/renamed-national-park.htm
  - Signed February 15, 2019. 15 miles of shoreline. Paul H. Douglas Trail.
- FAQ: https://www.nps.gov/indu/faqs.htm
  - 15,349 acres as of 2021. State park unchanged by the 2019 name. Not used for acreage.
- Basic information and homepage: 15 miles, more than 1,100 native plants, more than 50 miles of trails, about 16,000 acres as a round number.
- Audio brochure: port bargain. 8,330 acres grew to over 15,000.

The butterfly drawing is not identified to species. Fees and swim warnings are left to NPS.
