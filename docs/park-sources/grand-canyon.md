# Grand Canyon sources

Primary page read for this essay:

- https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/management/statistics.htm (NPS park statistics, updated July 28, 2026)

Facts taken from that page: park established February 26, 1919; 1,218,375 acres; 278 river miles; South Rim near 7,000 feet; North Rim near 8,000 feet; average depth about one mile; average rim-to-rim width about 10 miles, maximum 18; Phantom Ranch elevation about 2,400 feet; rock ages about 1.8 billion to 230 million years; river course about 6 million years; average gradient about 7 feet per mile; 91 mammals; about 450 birds; 18 fish, 5 native; 11 associated tribes; 1893 forest reserve; 1908 monument; 1979 World Heritage Site.

The 2025 Dragon Bravo Fire and current facility status were deliberately left out of the evergreen essay. Check NPS for conditions.
