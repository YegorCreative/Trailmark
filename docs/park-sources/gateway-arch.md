# Gateway Arch sources

Live pages used:

- Purpose and significance: https://www.nps.gov/jeff/learn/news/gateway-arch-national-park-purpose-and-significance.htm
  - 1935 executive order. Saarinen. Groundbreaking June 1959. Completed October 1965. Redesignated a national park in 2018 without changing operations, boundaries, or mission.
- Basic information: https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm
  - NPS unit since 1935, Executive Order 7253, 40 city blocks. Renamed in 2018. Security checkpoint. Tram tickets through the park partner. Museums described as free.
- Brochure: https://www.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/park-brochure.htm
  - 630 feet by 630 feet. Sway up to 18 inches. 30 miles in ideal conditions. 91 acres. 172 proposals. Construction February 12, 1963 to October 28, 1965. Opened June 9, 1967. Construction cost figures as used in the essay.
- History and culture: https://www.nps.gov/jeff/historyculture/index.htm
  - Competition 1947–48. Completed October 28, 1965, less than 15 million dollars. Foundations 60 feet. Old Courthouse begun 1839. Dred Scott trials 1847 and 1850. Reopened May 3, 2025. Acreage 90.96.
- Foundation document PDF: Public Law 115-128, approved February 22, 2018. Executive order dated December 21, 1935.
- FAQ: https://www.nps.gov/jeff/faqs.htm
  - Bill signed in February 2018. National Historic Landmark 1987, 62 acres. Dan Kiley landscape.

The title hook is "Steel Arch" because "Stainless Steel Arch" made the title 61 characters. Tram prices are left to NPS. The tram drawing is the ride, not a vehicle count.
