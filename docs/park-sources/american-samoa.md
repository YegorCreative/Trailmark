# National Park of American Samoa sources

Live pages opened:

- History and culture, last updated September 27, 2024: https://www.nps.gov/npsa/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Established in 1993. More than 8,000 acres on Tutuila, Ofu, and Ta‘ū.
  - Only paleotropical rainforest in the U.S. national park system. Over 475 plant species and 50 animal species, including two native fruit bats (pe‘a).
  - Only U.S. national park with an Indo-Pacific coral reef. More than 230 coral species and over 900 fish species. Ofu Lagoon noted for temperature resistance.
  - Land leased from villages under matai. Fa‘asamoa. People about 3,000 years. Saua on Ta‘ū called the birthplace of Polynesian culture.
  - Islands are peaks of underwater volcanoes. Mt. Lata named the highest point in American Samoa. No elevation printed.
- Nature and science, last updated September 27, 2024: https://www.nps.gov/npsa/learn/nature/index.htm
  - Nearly 4,000 acres of ocean. Same coral and fish totals. Green and hawksbill sea turtles. Humpback whales in the austral winter.
  - Pressures: climate change, overfishing, invasive species, bleaching, storms, crown-of-thorns starfish.
