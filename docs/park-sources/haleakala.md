# Haleakalā sources

Live pages used:

- Centennial: https://www.nps.gov/hale/learn/haleakala-centennial.htm
  - Part of Hawaiʻi National Park, August 1, 1916. Separate park in 1961. Summit 10,023 feet. Centennial acreage 33,265. Kīpahulu added in the story. ʻĀhinahina. Jaggar on the sunrise.
- Timeline: https://www.nps.gov/hale/learn/historyculture/timeline.htm
  - 1961 split into Haleakalā and Hawaiʻi Volcanoes. Army closure 1941–1943. Nēnē reintroduction 1962–1978. Kīpahulu authorized 1951. Fencing began 1976.
- Management, last updated February 6, 2025: https://www.nps.gov/hale/learn/management/index.htm
  - 30,183 acres. Wilderness 24,719 acres.
- Foundation overview PDF: summit 10,023 feet. Crater about 20 miles around. Rim more than 2,500 feet in places. About 24,000 acres wilderness.
- Legislation quoted on the Hawaiʻi Volcanoes page: https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/management/19600913_fed_law.htm
  - Haleakalā established as a separate unit effective July 1, 1961. Act approved September 13, 1960.
- Highway history: https://www.nps.gov/hale/learn/historyculture/hale-highway.htm
  - Ranch trade 1928. Road opened 1935. Crater hidden until the top.

No statistics page. Acreage uses more than 30,000, which both 30,183 and 33,265 support. Wilderness uses about 24,000, which both 24,719 and about 24,000 support. Summit 10,023 is shared.
