# Acadia sources

No NPS page was opened for this batch. The essay uses stable, widely documented history and avoids acreage and elevation numbers.

Claims that still need a direct NPS check:

- Established 1919 as Lafayette National Park and renamed Acadia in 1929
- First national park east of the Mississippi
- Cadillac Mountain as the highest U.S. Atlantic seaboard summit
- Carriage roads associated with John D. Rockefeller Jr.
- Park units on Mount Desert Island, Schoodic Peninsula, and Isle au Haut
- Somes Sound described as a fjard

Starting points for the review: https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm and https://www.nps.gov/acad/learn/historyculture/index.htm
