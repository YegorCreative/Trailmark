# Theodore Roosevelt sources

Live pages used:

- Park statistics: https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/management/statistics.htm
  - Acreage by unit: South Unit 46,158.57, North Unit 24,070.32, Elkhorn Ranch Unit 218, total 70,446.89 acres. Wilderness 29,920 acres. Designation history: 1935 Roosevelt Recreation Demonstration Area; November 1946 Theodore Roosevelt National Wildlife Refuge; April 25, 1947 Theodore Roosevelt National Memorial Park; November 10, 1978 Theodore Roosevelt National Park. Visitation nearly 600,000/year, peak June-August.
- History and culture index: https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Roosevelt ranched in Dakota Territory in the 1880s cattle boom; Maltese Cross and Elkhorn ranches both located within the current park.
- Horses of Theodore Roosevelt National Park: https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/nature/horses.htm
  - As of October 2024, approximately 198 horses in the South Unit; 179 captured in the October roundup. GPS collar tracking on 15 mares (about 70% of herd movement data). Management uses GonaCon immuno-contraception; five mares released from treatment for genetic diversity. No set target population; future herd size expected to be lower but undetermined pending Population Viability Analysis.
- WebSearch summary of NPS results re: bison: first release December 1956, 12 yearling heifers, 12 mature cows, 5 young bulls (29 total) from Fort Niobrara National Wildlife Refuge, Nebraska. North Unit release delayed by road construction and inholdings until November 1962, when 20 animals transferred from the South Unit herd. Since 1956, 3,752 bison relocated from the park in total.
