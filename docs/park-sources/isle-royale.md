# Isle Royale sources

Live pages used:

- Foundation document (2016 PDF): https://www.nps.gov/isro/learn/management/upload/ISRO_Web_NotAccessible_Foundation_Document_2016.pdf
  - Authorized March 3, 1931, Hoover. Established April 3, 1940, Roosevelt. 571,790 acres. 75 percent submerged. More than 400 islands. 14 miles from Canada, about 20 from Minnesota, about 45 from the Upper Peninsula. Wilderness October 20, 1976: 132,018 of 133,788 land acres. Wolf and moose together since the late 1940s. 1936 fire, 20 percent of the forests. No roads.
- Cultural history context PDF: dedication August 27, 1946. Albert Stoll.
- Homepage, last updated August 27, 2026: https://www.nps.gov/isro/
  - Minong, the good place, Grand Portage Band. Ancestral Anishinaabe land. Moose. Four lighthouses, including Rock of Ages.
- History index: https://www.nps.gov/isro/learn/historyculture/index.htm
  - Copper, fisheries, lighthouses, logging, CCC, resorts, cottages.

The moose drawing is not a census. Boat schedules and food-storage rules are left to NPS. The three mainland distances are different shores, not one disputed figure.
