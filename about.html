About TrailMark

A project built to celebrate and explore America’s National Parks — one badge at a time.

TrailMark is a digital field guide to America’s 63 National Parks. Each park gets its own illustrated badge, curated facts, and a dedicated page documenting the landscapes, wildlife, and stories that make it remarkable. The goal is a reference worth returning to — not a database to scroll past.

This is an ongoing project. Parks are added gradually, with care given to each badge design and write-up. Yosemite, Yellowstone, and Everglades are the published park pages. All 63 parks are planned for future updates.

TrailMark has no affiliation with the National Park Service or any government agency. It is an independent project made for anyone who loves the American wilderness.
